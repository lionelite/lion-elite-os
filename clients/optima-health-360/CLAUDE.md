# Claude Code Task — Optima Health 360 Affiliate Pack

Work only inside `clients/optima-health-360/` unless a reusable document-generation helper clearly belongs elsewhere.

## Objective
Produce the final client-ready Optima Health 360 Affiliate Content System for a $250 engagement. Keep scope tight and professional.

## Source of truth
Read `README.md` first.
The original client source is the Gmail email titled `Affiliate Content Template Info` and its attachments.

## Required final output
A polished PDF, approximately 12–18 pages, that includes:
1. Optima branding/logo.
2. The exact supplied product image beside each corresponding product section whenever that exact asset exists.
3. For each major product: 3 hooks, 1 ready-to-post caption, 1 Reel/TikTok idea, 1 Story idea, key talking points, CTA, and Do/Don't guidance.
4. 10% first-order affiliate offer and `[AFFILIATE CODE]` / `[AFFILIATE LINK]` placeholders.
5. One simple How To Use page.
6. One universal compliance/affiliate-disclosure page.
7. Quick DM/comment reply scripts.

## Hard asset rules
- Exact product -> exact product/strength image only.
- Never substitute a different vial image.
- If no exact image exists, use a clearly labeled `ASSET NEEDED` box.
- Do not invent Optima branding or alter product labels.

## Research-product content rules
- No dosing.
- No reconstitution instructions.
- No personal-use protocols.
- No treatment/cure claims.
- No guaranteed human outcomes.
- Keep research-product content framed around research interest, mechanisms at a high level, evidence literacy, and catalog discovery.

## Scope ceiling
Do not turn this into a full agency project. Do not create a 30/60/90 day calendar, custom video production, dozens of bespoke graphics, deep scientific literature reviews, or ongoing management.

## QA before finalizing
- All pages render without clipping/overlap.
- Product name matches image label.
- Prices match `README.md`.
- 10% offer is consistent.
- No mismatched images.
- No forbidden claims/instructions.
- Output feels polished enough to justify $250 but remains efficient.

## Deliverables to commit
- `source/affiliate-playbook.md` or equivalent editable source.
