## Language Standard

Use present-tense and embodied language whenever describing Lion Elite's goals, future, identity, business growth, wealth, impact, relationships, health, discipline, and opportunities.

Prefer:

- We have.
- We are.
- This is our reality.
- We operate at this level.
- We create value at scale.
- Opportunities flow toward us because we are prepared to recognize and execute on them.
- Our businesses are profitable, organized, scalable, and impactful.
- We are surrounded by the right people, partners, clients, and opportunities.

Avoid defaulting to:

- We hope.
- We wish.
- Maybe one day.
- We want to become.
- We will eventually.

Future tense can still be used for concrete scheduling and implementation details, but identity, belief, and vision language should be grounded in present reality.

## Daily Visualization Protocol

### 1. Alignment and Gratitude — 2 minutes

Begin by recognizing alignment with the one true God. Name three things that are already abundant in life now. Feel them instead of merely listing them.

### 2. Identity — 2 minutes

Ask:

- Who am I when this reality is normal?
- How do I think?
- How do I speak?
- How do I carry myself?
- What do I refuse to tolerate?
- How do my actions demonstrate faith, integrity, stewardship, and purpose?

### 3. Visualization — 3 minutes

Mentally experience the desired reality in first person. See the rooms, people, numbers, conversations, businesses, health, relationships, freedom, and daily routine. Focus on sensory detail and the emotional state of already being there.

### 4. Embodied Statement — 1 minute

Say out loud:

> We are aligned with the one true God. We are in tune with the one true God. Through God, we have limitless power. We have it. We are living it. Now we execute in alignment with it.

### 5. Abundance Action — 2 minutes

Choose the single action today that most strongly proves alignment with the reality being visualized.

Examples:

- Make the important sales call.
- Follow up with the high-value lead.
- Create the content.
- Improve the product.
- Solve the operational bottleneck.
- Train.
- Study.
- Save or invest capital.
- Have the difficult conversation.
- Serve someone exceptionally well.

## Scarcity-to-Abundance Reframes

- "There are not enough customers." → "There are millions of people with problems we are capable of helping solve. Our job is to reach them and create undeniable value."
- "We need money." → "We create value, build assets, improve our offers, strengthen distribution, and position ourselves for capital."
- "The opportunity is gone." → "Opportunity is abundant. We learn, adapt, and recognize the next opening faster."
- "Someone else is ahead of us." → "Their success expands our evidence of what is possible. We study what works and execute our own path."
- "We failed." → "We received data. We improve the system and move forward stronger."
- "I am overwhelmed." → "I know the next highest-value action, and I complete it now."
