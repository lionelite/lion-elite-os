# Customer Communication Rules

These rules apply to all Lion Elite customer-facing emails, DMs, SMS, follow-ups, captions, and sales messages.

## Core Tone

All customer communication must feel human, warm, relationship-building, and customer-service focused.

Do not sound robotic, scripted, pushy, overly corporate, or like a generic automation.

The customer should feel guided, heard, and personally supported before they feel sold to.

## Email and Follow-Up Standard

Every email should:

- Start with a natural, human opening.
- Acknowledge the person or situation when possible.
- Sound like a real relationship is being built.
- Keep paragraphs short and easy to read.
- Explain the next step clearly.
- End with a natural question or simple next action.

Avoid:

- Cold robotic intros.
- Long corporate language.
- Overly aggressive urgency.
- Generic templates that sound copied and pasted.
- Pushing a product before understanding the person.

## Inventory Privacy Rule

Inventory is internal business data.

Do not send customers, leads, affiliates, or outside parties:

- Exact inventory lists.
- Unit counts.
- Product quantities.
- Batch details.
- Internal source-sheet details.
- Internal notes or adjustment notes.
- Screenshots of inventory unless explicitly approved for internal team use only.

Customer-facing availability language should only say:

- Limited stock.
- Current availability is limited.
- I can confirm current availability for you.
- I can help you check what makes sense before you order.

If something is out of stock, do not advertise it as available. Offer to confirm availability, guide them to a waitlist, or help them choose the right next step.

## Lion Elite Wellness Compliance

For Lion Elite Wellness, product communication must stay research-use-only.

Do not include:

- Dosing.
- Human-use instructions.
- Disease claims.
- Treatment claims.
- Transformation promises.
- Medical advice.
- Personal-use protocols.

Allowed style:

- For laboratory research purposes only.
- Not for human or veterinary use.
- Studied in controlled laboratory models.
- Current availability is limited; I can confirm what is available before you order.

## Example Customer-Facing Note

Hey — I appreciate you reaching out.

Current availability is limited right now, so I do not want to just throw random options at you. I would rather help you confirm what makes sense and point you in the right direction.

For Lion Elite Wellness, everything is for laboratory research purposes only and not for human or veterinary use.

What are you looking for help understanding first?
