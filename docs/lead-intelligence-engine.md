# Lion Elite Lead Intelligence Engine

## Goal
Build a public-business lead intelligence pipeline that discovers businesses, verifies information across multiple public professional sources, extracts website content, scores leads, and stores structured records for CRM and AI outreach.

## Sources
- Google Business Profiles
- Official business websites
- LinkedIn
- Instagram business profiles
- Facebook business pages
- Public business directories

## Workflow
1. Discover businesses.
2. Resolve identity across public business sources.
3. Crawl public business websites.
4. Extract business information and services.
5. Compute confidence and lead scores.
6. Save structured records.
7. Generate AI outreach drafts.

## Stored Fields
Business name, website, business email, business phone, city, state, social profiles, website summary, services, lead score, confidence score, verification history, last verified.
