# Lion Elite Core Sales Framework

## Authority and Scope

This framework is the required foundation for anything Lion Elite sells or communicates across Lion Elite Wellness, Lion Elite Beauty, BUNKER, real estate, outreach, AI agents, websites, landing pages, email, SMS, DMs, calls, scripts, offers, client onboarding, and future brands.

Claude Code and every Lion Elite agent must use this framework by default whenever creating, reviewing, or improving sales-related communication.

The goal is not to force a generic pitch. The goal is to understand the person quickly, focus only on what applies to their situation, establish value around their goals, and confidently guide them toward the right next step.

## Core Sales Sequence

### 1. Power Statement: Confidence

Begin with confidence, certainty, energy, and initiative.

People buy confidence before they buy a product or service. The representative should lead the conversation without sounding aggressive, desperate, apologetic, or uncertain.

Core traits:

- Confidence
- Goal orientation
- Energy
- Initiative
- Honest and direct communication

### 2. Explain How We Can Help

Give a clear, relevant explanation of how Lion Elite may help, but do not overload the person with features or information that may not apply.

Connect the offer to the outcome the person wants.

### 3. Ask Clarifying Questions

Determine where the person is as early as possible.

Use open-ended prompts such as:

- Tell me about...
- Explain...
- Describe...
- Walk me through...
- Help me understand...

Discover:

- Their current situation
- Their primary goal
- Why that goal matters
- Their biggest obstacle
- What they have already tried
- Their desired timeline
- Their level of commitment
- Their decision-making concerns
- What success looks like to them

Do not assume. Diagnose before prescribing.

### 4. Identify the Psychology Type

The four primary psychology types must be approached differently. Determine the person's dominant style as early as possible and communicate in the language they naturally respond to.

#### Performer

Responds to:

- Results
- Winning
- Recognition
- Status
- Speed
- Energy
- Transformation

Be energetic, confident, and outcome-focused. Show what success looks like and why this gives them an advantage.

#### Analyzer

Responds to:

- Logic
- Evidence
- Details
- Structure
- Comparisons
- Process
- Accuracy

Be organized, precise, and transparent. Explain why the solution makes sense and how the process works. Do not rush them or use empty hype.

#### Controller

Responds to:

- Control
- Efficiency
- Independence
- Directness
- Flexibility
- Clear expectations
- Fast decision-making

Be concise, honest, and direct. Respect their autonomy. Show them the most efficient path and allow them to feel in control of the decision.

#### Empathizer

Responds to:

- Trust
- Support
- Relationships
- Accountability
- Safety
- Community
- Personal attention

Be warm, patient, and genuine. Show that they will be supported and understood throughout the process.

## 5. Build Value Around Their Goal

Never give the same presentation to everyone.

Only discuss benefits that apply to the person's stated situation, goals, personality, obstacles, and priorities.

Every important point should answer:

> How does this help the client accomplish the goal they just described?

Do not say things that do not apply merely because they are part of a standard script.

Sell the relevant transformation, not a list of features.

**Price is only an issue in the absence of value.**

This does not mean hiding or manipulating price. It means establishing an honest, specific connection between the investment and the value of solving the person's actual problem before discussing fees.

## 6. Explain the Three-Step Process

Keep the process clear and simple so the person understands exactly what happens next.

1. **Assessment** — Understand where they are, what they want, and what is preventing progress.
2. **Personalized Strategy** — Match the correct solution, offer, or roadmap to their situation and psychology type.
3. **Execution and Support** — Guide implementation, accountability, communication, and necessary adjustments.

Adapt the names of the three steps to the specific brand or offer, but preserve this structure.

## 7. Present Fees

Discuss fees only after understanding the person's situation and establishing relevant value.

When presenting price:

- Be confident.
- Be direct.
- Do not apologize.
- Do not sound defensive.
- Do not pressure or deceive.
- Explain available payment structures clearly.
- Connect the investment back to the client's stated goal and value.

## 8. Close

Guide the person toward a clear decision and next step.

Examples:

- Based on what you told me, this is the option that best fits your goal.
- Are you ready to move forward?
- Let's get your application completed.
- Which payment option works best for you?
- Is there anything specific preventing you from getting started today?

When an objection appears, clarify it honestly. Return to the person's goals, priorities, concerns, and value rather than arguing or repeating a generic pitch.

## Required Communication Principles

- Lead with confidence.
- Focus on the client's goals.
- Understand before advising.
- Ask clarifying questions early.
- Identify the psychology type quickly.
- Adapt the communication style accordingly.
- Be honest and direct.
- Do not use irrelevant talking points.
- Do not overwhelm people with features.
- Personalize the value proposition.
- Explain a simple three-step path.
- Present fees confidently.
- Ask for the sale or next step.
- Never manufacture urgency, make false promises, or manipulate the client.
- If Lion Elite is not the right solution, say so honestly.

## Instructions for Claude Code and AI Agents

Whenever building or editing a sales-facing system, Claude must:

1. Treat this framework as the default sales logic.
2. Incorporate psychology-type detection into intake questions, sales agents, scripts, CRM notes, and AI prompts where relevant.
3. Avoid generic outputs that mention benefits unrelated to the specific lead.
4. Make clarifying questions part of the workflow before generating recommendations or offers.
5. Build value around the person's expressed goal before presenting fees.
6. Use honest, direct, confident language.
7. Keep the three-step process visible and easy to understand.
8. Include a clear close or next action in sales-facing communication.
9. Preserve compliance, consent, privacy, and all hard limits in `CLAUDE.md`.
10. Flag any existing sales workflow that conflicts with this framework and propose a specific correction.

## Default Sales Flow for AI

When context is limited, use this order:

1. Confident power statement
2. Brief explanation of how Lion Elite may help
3. Clarifying questions
4. Psychology-type classification
5. Personalized value explanation
6. Three-step process
7. Fees or payment options
8. Direct close

The AI should not skip discovery and jump immediately into an irrelevant pitch.
