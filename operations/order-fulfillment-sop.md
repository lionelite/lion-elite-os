# Order Fulfillment SOP

Source basis: Google Drive order and shipping sheets, including `website/A`, `Shipping and Tracking`, `Client directory`, and sales/commission sheets. These sheets track date, name, phone number, shipping address, total paid, product/quantity, tracking number, order status, and shipping details.

## Purpose
Create a repeatable process for taking a paid order from customer information to packed shipment, tracking update, and completed status.

## Required Information
Before fulfilling an order, confirm:

- Customer name
- Phone number
- Email if available
- Shipping address
- Product and quantity
- Total paid
- Payment status
- Order source
- Rep/source if commission applies

## Workflow

### 1. Confirm Payment
- Verify total paid.
- Confirm payment method cleared.
- Do not ship unpaid or unclear orders.

### 2. Confirm Products
- Match product and quantity to inventory.
- Confirm dosage and units.
- If item is unavailable, flag order as pending.

### 3. Prepare Shipping Details
- Confirm full address.
- Check spelling, apartment/unit numbers, city, state, and ZIP.
- Confirm phone/email if needed.

### 4. Pack Order
- Pull products.
- Confirm each product against order list.
- Package securely.
- Add any required inserts, labels, or notices.

### 5. Create Shipping Label
- Create label in shipping system.
- Save tracking number.
- Record carrier and service.
- Record shipping cost if available.

### 6. Update Tracking Sheet
Update the appropriate sheet or system with:

- Tracking number
- Shipping status
- Ship date
- Order status
- Notes

### 7. Notify Customer
Send customer tracking information.

Message template:

Hey [Name], your Lion Elite order has been processed and shipped.

Tracking: [Tracking Number]

Thank you for your order.

### 8. Mark Complete
Once tracking is created and customer is notified:

- Mark status as shipped.
- Later update to delivered when confirmed.

## Order Status Options

- Paid
- Pending Payment
- Processing
- Shipped
- Delivered
- Issue / Needs Review
- Refunded / Cancelled

## Quality Control Checklist

- [ ] Payment confirmed
- [ ] Customer information confirmed
- [ ] Product quantity confirmed
- [ ] Inventory updated
- [ ] Tracking number saved
- [ ] Customer notified
- [ ] Status updated

## Common Mistakes

- Shipping before payment clears
- Missing apartment/unit number
- Wrong product quantity
- Forgetting to update tracking
- Forgetting to update commission source
- Failing to update inventory after shipment

## Compliance Note
Do not include medical claims in customer shipping messages. Keep communications professional and order-focused.
