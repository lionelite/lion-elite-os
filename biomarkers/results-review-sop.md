# Biomarker Results Review SOP

## Purpose
Create a safe, professional process for reviewing biomarker results with Lion Elite Beauty clients.

## Scope
Lion Elite Beauty may use biomarker results for wellness education, coaching personalization, client awareness, lifestyle planning, and referral decisions.

Lion Elite Beauty should not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or tell clients to change medication unless a licensed provider is directly involved.

## Results Review Workflow

### 1. Confirm Results Received
Track:

- Client name
- Panel
- Collection method
- Date ordered
- Date collected
- Date results received

### 2. Internal Review
Review results for:

- Out-of-range markers
- Patterns related to client goals
- Markers needing provider review
- Missing markers
- Retesting needs

### 3. Flag Urgent or Concerning Results
If results appear clinically concerning, direct the client to:

- Platform physician
- Primary care physician
- Appropriate licensed healthcare provider

Do not attempt to manage urgent medical findings through coaching.

### 4. Prepare Client Summary
Use simple categories:

- Strong markers
- Markers to improve
- Lifestyle factors connected to markers
- Questions for provider
- Next steps for coaching plan

### 5. Client Review Call
Call format:

1. Confirm client's goals.
2. Explain that labs are reviewed for education and wellness planning.
3. Review major patterns.
4. Clarify what is outside coaching scope.
5. Adjust plan.
6. Set retesting schedule.

### 6. Document Follow-Up
Save:

- Date reviewed
- Key notes
- Referrals recommended
- Plan changes
- Retesting timeline

## Client-Facing Disclaimer Language
Your biomarker results are used to help personalize your wellness and coaching plan. Lion Elite Beauty does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe. Any abnormal or concerning results should be reviewed with the ordering physician, your primary care provider, or another qualified licensed healthcare professional.

## Review Categories

### Green
Markers look within expected range and do not require immediate attention.

### Yellow
Markers may benefit from lifestyle, nutrition, recovery, or coaching focus.

### Red
Markers should be reviewed with a licensed healthcare provider.

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Results received
- [ ] Out-of-range markers identified
- [ ] Red flags referred to provider
- [ ] Client summary created
- [ ] Review call completed
- [ ] Coaching plan adjusted
- [ ] Retest date set
