# Biomarker Panel Library

## Purpose
Create a simple panel system for Lion Elite Beauty so every program has a matching testing option.

## Recommended Starting Number
Start with 8 core panels.

This gives enough specificity without making the system too complicated.

---

# 1. Foundation Wellness Panel

## Best For
Every new client, general wellness, baseline assessment.

## Marker Categories
- CBC
- CMP
- Lipid panel
- HbA1c
- Fasting glucose
- TSH
- Vitamin D
- Iron panel / ferritin

## Use Case
Establishes a basic baseline before building a program.

---

# 2. Weight Loss & Metabolism Panel

## Best For
Fat loss, metabolic health, weight management clients.

## Marker Categories
- Foundation markers
- Fasting insulin
- HbA1c
- Glucose
- Lipids
- hs-CRP
- Thyroid markers
- Liver enzymes

## Use Case
Helps personalize fat loss strategy and identify metabolic bottlenecks.

---

# 3. Muscle & Performance Panel

## Best For
Muscle-building, strength, athletic performance, recovery.

## Marker Categories
- CBC
- CMP
- Testosterone
- Free testosterone
- SHBG
- Estradiol
- IGF-1
- Ferritin
- Vitamin D
- Kidney/liver markers

## Use Case
Supports performance, recovery, and training strategy.

---

# 4. Hormone Optimization Panel

## Best For
Men and women with hormone-related goals or concerns.

## Marker Categories
- Testosterone
- Free testosterone
- Estradiol
- SHBG
- DHEA-S
- LH
- FSH
- Prolactin
- TSH
- Free T3
- Free T4

## Use Case
Helps identify when hormone-related patterns may require provider review.

---

# 5. Longevity Panel

## Best For
High-end wellness, anti-aging, longevity, executive optimization.

## Marker Categories
- Advanced lipids
- ApoB
- Lp(a)
- hs-CRP
- Homocysteine
- HbA1c
- Fasting insulin
- Vitamin D
- Omega-3 index if available

## Use Case
Focuses on long-term risk, inflammation, and optimization.

---

# 6. Cognitive Performance Panel

## Best For
Focus, mood, stress, productivity, mental performance.

## Marker Categories

- Folate
- Vitamin D
- Iron/ferritin
- Thyroid markers
- Cortisol if available
- DHEA-S

## Use Case
Supports cognitive and energy-related coaching decisions.

---

# 7. Cardiovascular Panel

## Best For
Heart-health focused clients, high-risk clients, longevity clients.

## Marker Categories
- Lipid panel
- ApoB
- Lp(a)
- hs-CRP
- HbA1c

---

# 8. Comprehensive Elite Panel

## Best For
VIP, executive, premium, full optimization clients.

## Marker Categories
Combines:

- Foundation Wellness
- Weight Loss & Metabolism
- Hormone Optimization
- Longevity

---

## Notes

- Final exact biomarkers depend on what is available inside the lab platform.
- Panel pricing must be confirmed in the platform before quoting clients.
- Not all panels may be eligible for BioBox at-home collection.
- LabCorp draw is likely preferred for larger comprehensive panels.
