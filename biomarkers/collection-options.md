# Biomarker Collection Options

## Purpose
Explain the two main collection options for Lion Elite Beauty biomarker testing.

## Option 1: LabCorp Draw Center

### Best For
- Standard blood panels
- Clients comfortable going to a lab
- Larger panels that require venipuncture
- Clients who want a straightforward in-person draw

### Workflow
1. Order is submitted through the lab platform.
2. Platform physician authorizes the order.
3. Client receives instructions.
4. Client goes to participating LabCorp draw center.
5. LabCorp completes blood draw.
6. Results return through platform.

### Known Fee
- Approximate LabCorp draw/order fee: $8.

### Client-Facing Language
Your lab order will be authorized through the platform's physician network. You can complete your blood draw at a participating LabCorp location. A small draw/order fee may apply, currently expected to be around $8.

## Option 2: BioBox At-Home Collection

### Best For
- Convenience-focused clients
- Clients who prefer testing from home
- Eligible tests that support at-home collection
- Remote clients

### Workflow
1. Order is submitted through the platform.
2. Platform physician authorizes the order if required.
3. BioBox kit is shipped to the client.
4. Client completes collection using kit instructions.
5. Client returns sample as directed.
6. Results return through platform.

### Client-Facing Language
For eligible tests, you may be able to use BioBox, the at-home collection option through the lab platform partner. The kit is shipped to you with instructions for collection and return.

## Choosing the Right Collection Method

| Client Situation | Recommended Option |
|---|---|
| Wants fastest standard blood work | LabCorp |
| Needs full venipuncture panel | LabCorp |
| Wants convenience from home | BioBox |
| Lives far from draw center | BioBox if eligible |
| Large comprehensive panel | LabCorp |
| Simple eligible test | BioBox may work |

## Internal Notes

- Not every biomarker panel may qualify for BioBox.
- Confirm availability inside the lab platform before promising at-home testing.
- Always clarify whether fees are paid by Lion Elite Beauty, passed to client, or included in program cost.
