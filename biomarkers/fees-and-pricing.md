# Biomarker Testing Fees and Pricing

## Known Platform Fees

### Physician Authorization Fee
- Approximate fee: $5 per order.
- Paid for use of the platform's physician network.
- This allows Lion Elite Beauty to begin without its own NPI if the platform physician network is used.

### LabCorp Draw Fee
- Approximate fee: $8 per order/draw.
- Applies when the client uses a LabCorp draw center.

### BioBox At-Home Collection
- Fee depends on platform and kit/test type.
- Confirm inside platform before quoting final price.

## Internal Pricing Decision Needed
Lion Elite Beauty must decide whether these fees are:

1. Included in the program price.
2. Added to the client invoice.
3. Paid separately by client through the platform.
4. Absorbed by Lion Elite for VIP clients.

## Recommended Pricing Structure

### Basic Program Clients
Client pays testing cost plus applicable platform/draw fees.

### Premium Program Clients
Include physician authorization fee in program price, but client pays draw/kit fee if charged separately.

### VIP Clients
Include physician authorization fee and LabCorp draw fee inside program price for a premium experience.

## Example Client Explanation
Your biomarker testing order is authorized through the lab platform's physician network. There may be a small physician authorization fee, currently expected around $5, and if you use LabCorp for collection, a draw/order fee around $8 may apply.

## Internal Margin Planning
When pricing biomarker testing, account for:

- Lab panel cost
- Physician authorization fee
- Draw/collection fee
- Admin time
- Results review time
- Follow-up consultation time
- Platform processing fees
- Payment processing fees

## Recommended Rule
Do not quote final biomarker pricing until the exact panel is selected inside the platform.

## Tracking Fields
For every biomarker order, track:

- Client name
- Program
- Panel selected
- Lab panel cost
- Physician fee
- Draw fee
- BioBox fee if applicable
- Total client charge
- Gross margin
- Payment status
