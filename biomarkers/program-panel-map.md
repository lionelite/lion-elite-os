# Program to Biomarker Panel Map

## Purpose
Match each Lion Elite Beauty program to the correct biomarker testing panel.

| Lion Elite Beauty Program | Recommended Panel | Collection Preference |
|---|---|---|
| General Wellness | Foundation Wellness Panel | LabCorp or BioBox if eligible |
| Fat Loss / Weight Loss | Weight Loss & Metabolism Panel | LabCorp preferred |
| Muscle Building | Muscle & Performance Panel | LabCorp preferred |
| Athletic Performance | Muscle & Performance Panel | LabCorp preferred |
| Hormone Optimization | Hormone Optimization Panel | LabCorp preferred |
| Longevity | Longevity Panel | LabCorp preferred |
| Cognitive Performance | Cognitive Performance Panel | LabCorp or BioBox if eligible |
| Cardiovascular Health | Cardiovascular Panel | LabCorp preferred |
| VIP / Executive Optimization | Comprehensive Elite Panel | LabCorp preferred |

## Intake Questions by Program

### General Wellness
- What made you want to test your biomarkers now?
- What do you want to improve first?
- Do you have any recent lab work?

### Fat Loss
- What is your current weight and goal weight?
- Have you struggled with cravings, energy, or plateaus?
- Have you tested glucose, insulin, thyroid, or lipids before?

### Muscle / Performance
- Are you focused on size, strength, endurance, recovery, or body composition?
- How often do you train?
- How is your sleep and recovery?

### Hormone Optimization
- What symptoms or goals made you interested in hormones?
- Have you tested hormones before?
- Are you currently working with a provider?

### Longevity
- Are you focused on prevention, performance, inflammation, or long-term health?
- Do you have family history of cardiovascular or metabolic issues?
- How often do you want to retest?

### Cognitive Performance
- Are you struggling more with focus, fatigue, mood, or stress?
- How is your sleep?
- Do you use caffeine or stimulants frequently?

### Cardiovascular
- Do you have a personal or family history of heart disease, high blood pressure, diabetes, or cholesterol issues?
- Have you tested ApoB or Lp(a) before?
- Are you under a physician's care?

## Internal Rule
If the client has significant symptoms, diagnosed conditions, alarming results, or medication questions, refer to the platform physician or their personal healthcare provider.
