# Biomarker Compliance Boundaries

## Purpose
Protect Lion Elite Beauty by clearly separating wellness coaching from medical care.

## What Lion Elite Beauty Can Do

Lion Elite Beauty can:

- Help clients choose a wellness-focused biomarker panel.
- Coordinate testing through the lab platform.
- Explain what biomarkers generally relate to.
- Use results to personalize coaching, nutrition, training, recovery, and lifestyle recommendations.
- Encourage clients to review abnormal results with a licensed provider.
- Track progress and retesting.
- Provide education and accountability.

## What Lion Elite Beauty Should Not Do

Lion Elite Beauty should not:

- Diagnose disease.
- Treat disease.
- Prescribe medication.
- Tell clients to start, stop, or change medication.
- Claim a product cures, treats, or prevents disease.
- Replace a doctor or licensed provider.
- Interpret urgent or dangerous results as a medical provider.

## Required Referral Situations

Refer client to a licensed healthcare provider when:

- Results are significantly out of range.
- Client has symptoms that may require medical evaluation.
- Client asks medication questions.
- Client has a diagnosed medical condition.
- Client is pregnant or trying to become pregnant.
- Client has cardiovascular, kidney, liver, cancer, endocrine, or autoimmune concerns.
- Client needs diagnosis or treatment.

## Approved Positioning

Use language like:

- Biomarker-informed wellness planning
- Lab-guided coaching
- Data-driven wellness support
- Personalized lifestyle strategy
- Health optimization education
- Results should be reviewed with a licensed provider when clinically relevant

## Avoid Language Like

- We treat low testosterone
- We cure fatigue
- We diagnose thyroid issues
- This protocol fixes diabetes
- This replaces your doctor
- Your labs show you have [diagnosis]

## Client Disclaimer

Lion Elite Beauty provides wellness education and coaching support. Biomarker testing is ordered through the lab platform and its physician network when required. Lion Elite Beauty does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or replace medical care. Clients should review abnormal or concerning results with the ordering physician or their personal healthcare provider.

## Internal Rule
When unsure, refer out.
