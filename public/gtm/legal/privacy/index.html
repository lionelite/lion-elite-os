BuildPipeline Privacy Notice

Effective September 24, 2026

Information we process

We process account identifiers, workspace configuration, billing references, campaign/prospect data supplied by customers or connected providers, product usage, connector metadata and operational logs necessary to provide the service.

How we use information

Information is used to provide and secure the product, process billing, operate customer-requested workflows, diagnose failures, enforce limits and improve service reliability.

Service providers

BuildPipeline may rely on infrastructure, payment, email, AI, data and integration providers to deliver requested features. Those providers process data according to their own contractual and privacy obligations.

Retention

Operational and account data is retained as needed to provide the service, maintain security, meet billing/legal requirements and resolve disputes. Retention periods may vary by data type and provider.

Customer data

Customers remain responsible for data they upload or connect and for ensuring they have the right to process it.

Contact

Contact BuildPipeline Support for privacy questions or requests.
