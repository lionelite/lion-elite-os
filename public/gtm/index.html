FAQ

Questions before you start.

Will anything send without my approval?

Each campaign can run in Supervised or Autopilot mode. Either way, messages still pass suppression, reply-stop, pacing, sender-health and compliance checks.

Do I need a list?

No. BuildPipeline supports provider-backed sourcing and enrichment, and you can also import your own CSV list.

How does BuildPipeline decide who is warm?

Intent is based on stored signals and campaign evidence, not open/click vanity metrics. Thresholds decide who enters the queue.

Can this run for clients?

Yes. Agency includes isolated client workspaces, membership roles, branding, usage limits and rollup reporting.

Is LinkedIn automation risk-free?

No. Automated LinkedIn activity can create platform/account risk. BuildPipeline keeps that channel provider-based and opt-in, with limits you can lower.
