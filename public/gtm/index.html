QUESTIONS

Know what you are buying.

Does LionOS send anything without control?

Campaigns support supervised and autopilot modes. Either way, delivery still passes the same compliance, suppression, sender-health, pacing and reply-stop rules.

Do I need my own prospect list?

No. LionOS includes provider-ready prospect sourcing and enrichment, and it also accepts customer CSV lists. Live data volume depends on the providers connected to the workspace.

What happens when credits run out?

The relevant metered work pauses at the allowance unless overage has been explicitly enabled. Usage is visible by credit purse and action.

Can I run clients?

Yes. Agency is designed around isolated client workspaces, role-based membership, branding, usage caps and cross-workspace reporting.

What needs to be connected?

Email/calendar OAuth, prospect data and sender infrastructure are connected per production workspace. LinkedIn automation, when used through a configured provider, carries platform/account risk and remains opt-in.
