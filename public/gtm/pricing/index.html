Billing

Know how the bill works.

What are data credits?

Data credits cover metered prospect/enrichment actions such as contact reveals and company enrichment.

What are action credits?

Action credits cover metered AI and workflow actions such as research, messaging, classification and sends.

What happens at the limit?

The relevant metered work stops at the allowance unless overage has been explicitly enabled.

Can I cancel?

Your subscription lifecycle is tracked through Stripe. Cancellation stops the recurring subscription and the workspace follows the billing state.
