# Lion Elite Wellness Sales Script

Source basis: Google Drive file `LION ELITE WELLNESS COACHING SCRIPT`, which opens with a Lion Elite Wellness coaching sales script and includes engage, power statement, and transformation-focused positioning.

## Purpose
Give Lion Elite a repeatable sales call structure for leads interested in improving health, physique, performance, energy, or overall quality of life.

## Call Structure

### 1. Engage
Hey [Name], this is Alex with Lion Elite Wellness.

I'm reaching out because you expressed interest in improving your health, physique, performance, or overall quality of life, and I wanted to see what you're looking to accomplish.

**Question:** What are you most focused on improving right now?

### 2. Power Statement
At Lion Elite Wellness, most people come to us because they know they need a change, but they are frustrated trying to figure everything out on their own.

Our clients work with us because we help provide roadmap, accountability, structure, and support so they can bridge the gap between where they are and where they want to be.

This may include losing body fat, building muscle, increasing energy, improving consistency, improving health markers, or becoming healthier for family and long-term performance.

**Question:** What would change in your life if you fixed this over the next 3 to 6 months?

### 3. Identify
Ask these questions:

- What is your main goal?
- What have you already tried?
- What worked?
- What failed?
- What is your current strategy?
- What is your timeframe?
- What happens if nothing changes?

### 4. Build Value
Position Lion Elite as the system, not just the product.

Key value points:

- Roadmap
- Accountability
- Product education
- Support
- Follow-up
- Strategy
- Progress tracking
- Long-term optimization mindset

### 5. Handle Objections

#### Price
I understand. Price matters, but usually price becomes the main issue when the value is not clear yet.

The real question is whether staying where you are is costing you more than fixing it.

#### Time
That is exactly why structure matters. If your time is limited, you need a clearer plan, not more confusion.

#### Need to Think About It
That makes sense. What part do you feel you need to think through: the investment, the plan, or whether this is the right time?

### 6. Close
The next step is simple:

1. Establish the plan.
2. Personalize the process.
3. Capitalize with ongoing support.

**Closing Question:** Based on what you told me, do you want help putting this plan together and getting started?

## Compliance Note
Keep language professional. Avoid promising medical outcomes, cures, or disease treatment. Position support as education, wellness planning, and accountability where appropriate.
