# Discovery Before the Pitch

## Primary Objective

The single most important objective in every sales conversation is to discover **why the person reached out**.

Before discussing services, pricing, products, or solutions, determine the reason behind their outreach.

## Core Discovery Questions

- Why are you reaching out today?
- What is your goal?
- Why is achieving this goal important to you?
- Why now?
- What changed that made you decide to take action today?

## The Principle

People rarely buy because they need more information.

They buy because something in their life has created enough pain, urgency, frustration, or desire for change that they are finally ready to take action.

Our job is to identify that reason before presenting a solution.

Once we understand:

- Their current situation
- Their desired outcome
- Why they are acting now
- What is preventing them from reaching their goal

...we build value around the exact problem they are trying to solve.

## Non-Negotiable Rule

**Never pitch before understanding the reason for the outreach.**

The client's motivation determines the direction of the entire conversation.

> **Diagnose before prescribing.**
