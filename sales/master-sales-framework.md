# Lion Elite Master Sales Framework

## Purpose

This document establishes the universal sales system for every Lion Elite service, partnership, outreach campaign, and AI-assisted sales workflow.

The operating principle is simple:

> Diagnose before prescribing. Build value around the prospect’s stated goals, then present the solution that genuinely fits.

Every representative, script, AI agent, CRM workflow, and outreach system should follow this structure.

---

## 1. Master Sales Framework

The following conversation sequence applies to every Lion Elite service.

### Step 1 — Power Statement

Open with confidence, relevance, and a clear reason for the conversation.

The power statement should:

- Establish immediate relevance.
- Communicate the category of value Lion Elite provides.
- Create enough curiosity to earn the next question.
- Avoid turning into a premature pitch.

### Step 2 — Discovery Before Prescription

Do not recommend a product, service, partnership, or system until the prospect’s situation is understood.

Discovery should identify:

- Their current position.
- Their desired outcome.
- The primary obstacle between the two.
- What they have already tried.
- Their urgency and timeframe.
- Their decision-making process.
- The cost of leaving the problem unresolved.

### Step 3 — Credibility in Under 15 Seconds

Credibility should be concise and directly connected to the prospect’s situation.

It may include:

- Relevant experience.
- A specific business capability.
- A comparable client result.
- A clear explanation of Lion Elite’s operating model.

Credibility earns permission to continue the conversation. It does not replace discovery and does not automatically earn the sale.

### Step 4 — Clarifying Questions

Use open-ended questions that encourage the prospect to explain, describe, and evaluate their own situation.

Preferred question formats include:

- “Tell me about…”
- “Walk me through…”
- “Explain how you currently…”
- “Describe what happens when…”
- “What would need to change for…”
- “Why is that important to you now?”

The prospect should speak more than the salesperson.

### Step 5 — Build Value Around Their Goals

Value must be connected to what the prospect actually said.

Do not introduce benefits that do not apply to their goals, problems, priorities, personality, or buying criteria.

Value should be expressed through:

- Desired outcomes.
- Time saved.
- Revenue gained or protected.
- Administrative work removed.
- Risk reduced.
- Client experience improved.
- Operational consistency created.
- Confidence, clarity, or accountability increased.

Price becomes a larger issue when value has not been clearly established.

### Step 6 — Present the Appropriate Solution

Present only the solution that matches the diagnosis.

The presentation should explain:

1. What will be implemented.
2. Why it fits the prospect’s situation.
3. How the process works.
4. What outcome the process is designed to support.
5. What participation is required from the prospect.
6. The applicable investment, fees, or revenue structure.

### Step 7 — Close and Next Steps

The close should be the logical continuation of the discovery conversation.

Confirm:

- The agreed goal.
- The recommended solution.
- Any remaining concern.
- The decision or commitment required.
- The exact next action.
- The owner and deadline for that action.

Do not end conversations with vague promises to reconnect. Every qualified conversation should conclude with a defined next step.

## 2. Business AI Scaling Platform

### Positioning

The Business AI Scaling Platform helps businesses use AI, automation, and organized operating systems to scale without adding unnecessary administrative burden.

### Core Outcomes

- Automate repetitive business processes.
- Remove administrative work from owners and employees.
- Improve lead management and follow-up.
- Create more consistent operations.
- Reduce preventable errors and lost opportunities.
- Increase profitability through efficiency and better execution.
- Give leadership clearer reporting and decision support.

### Business Owner Discovery Questions

- What part of the business currently depends too heavily on you?
- Which repetitive tasks consume the most employee time?
- Where are leads, customers, or follow-ups currently being lost?
- What systems are your team using today?
- Which processes are still being handled manually?
- Where do communication breakdowns happen?
- What would you scale first if operations were no longer the bottleneck?
- How are you measuring productivity, conversion, retention, and profitability?
- What is the financial impact of the current inefficiency?
- What would a successful implementation need to accomplish in the first 30, 60, and 90 days?

### Solution Categories

- AI-assisted lead intelligence.
- CRM organization and lifecycle automation.
- Automated follow-up and task creation.
- Customer support assistance.
- Internal knowledge systems.
- Reporting and KPI dashboards.
- Appointment and workflow automation.
- Sales enablement and conversation guidance.
- Human-reviewed content and outreach drafting.

Automation must support human judgment rather than remove accountability from high-impact decisions.

## 3. Personal Trainer Outreach

### Objective

Help personal trainers and online coaches identify operational problems that limit growth, retention, client experience, and profitability.

### Coach-Specific Discovery

- How many active clients are you currently managing?
- How are new leads entering your business?
- What happens after someone expresses interest?
- How quickly are leads followed up with?
- How are programs, check-ins, progress, and communication managed?
- What takes the most time outside of actual coaching?
- Where do clients most commonly disengage or cancel?
- How consistent is your onboarding experience?
- How are referrals, renewals, and upsells handled?
- What would prevent you from doubling your client base today?

### Scaling Coaching Businesses

Potential solutions may include:

- Lead capture and qualification workflows.
- Automated but personalized follow-up.
- Client onboarding systems.
- Check-in reminders and progress tracking.
- Client portal organization.
- Monthly call scheduling controls.
- Retention and renewal workflows.
- Referral systems.
- Administrative task automation.
- Reporting for client engagement and business performance.

The goal is not simply to help coaches acquire more clients. The goal is to help them serve more clients without reducing service quality.

## 4. Coach Partnership Program

### Purpose

The Coach Partnership Program creates qualified collaboration opportunities between coaches and Lion Elite Wellness.

### Partnership Positioning

The program may support:

- Research-focused educational collaboration.
- Lawful product referral relationships.
- Audience education.
- Co-branded campaigns where appropriate.
- Revenue-sharing opportunities.
- Long-term strategic partnerships.

### Qualification Process

Evaluate:

- Audience alignment.
- Professional reputation.
- Coaching experience.
- Communication quality.
- Compliance awareness.
- Current client volume.
- Engagement quality rather than follower count alone.
- Ability to educate without making unsupported claims.
- Willingness to follow Lion Elite brand and compliance standards.
- Long-term partnership potential.

### Discovery Questions

- Tell me about the clients you currently serve.
- What questions do your clients most frequently ask about wellness and research compounds?
- How do you currently evaluate partnership opportunities?
- What type of collaboration would provide genuine value to your audience?
- How do you currently generate revenue outside of direct coaching?
- What would make a partnership worth your time?
- How do you maintain trust when recommending an outside company?

### Revenue Share Structure

Any revenue-share arrangement must clearly define:

- Eligible products or services.
- Attribution method.
- Commission or revenue-share percentage.
- Treatment of discounts, refunds, fees, taxes, shipping, and product costs.
- Payment schedule.
- Required disclosures.
- Brand and compliance obligations.
- Circumstances that may terminate the partnership.

The specific commercial terms must be documented separately and approved before launch.

## 5. B2C Lead Acquisition Framework

### Objective

Identify prospective customers who are publicly expressing relevant interest in fitness, wellness, coaching, transformation, accountability, or related services, then begin a respectful discovery-first conversation.

### Permitted Information Sources

Use lawful, publicly available information and authorized first-party data, including where appropriate:

- Public social posts and comments.
- Public business or professional profiles.
- Voluntary form submissions.
- Website inquiries.
- Event registrations.
- Existing customer referrals.
- Authorized platform integrations.
- Publicly listed business contact information.

### Compliance Requirements

Prospecting workflows must respect:

- Applicable privacy and marketing laws.
- Platform terms and channel-specific rules.
- Website terms and robots directives where applicable.
- Consent and disclosure requirements.
- Suppression and do-not-contact lists.
- Data minimization principles.
- Source attribution and freshness tracking.
- Human review before high-impact outreach decisions.

The system must not rely on unrestricted data scraping, hidden personal data, deceptive identities, guessed private contact information, or unauthorized access.

### Prospect Signals

Relevant signals may include public statements indicating:

- A desire to lose weight, gain muscle, improve fitness, or increase accountability.
- Frustration with inconsistent progress.
- Interest in structured coaching.
- Difficulty maintaining habits.
- Interest in wellness education.
- A request for trainer, coach, or program recommendations.
- A recent life event that publicly prompted a new health or fitness goal.

A signal indicates potential relevance, not automatic qualification.

### Discovery-First Outreach

Initial outreach should:

- Reference the public context naturally.
- Avoid invasive or overly specific language.
- Ask a relevant question.
- Give the prospect an easy opportunity to decline.
- Avoid presenting a full offer before understanding the need.

### Matching Prospects to Services

Based on discovery, prospects may be matched to:

- Lion Elite Beauty coaching and accountability programs.
- Lion Elite Beauty wellness education and support services.
- Lion Elite Wellness research-focused products and educational content, subject to all applicable compliance requirements.
- No offer, when the prospect is not a genuine fit.

Fit is more important than forcing every lead into a sale.

## 6. Universal Sales Rules

1. Never pitch first.
2. Diagnose before prescribing.
3. The prospect should talk more than the salesperson.
4. Credibility earns the next question, not the sale.
5. Every service follows the same core conversation framework.
6. Ask questions that uncover goals, problems, urgency, impact, and decision criteria.
7. Do not mention benefits that do not apply to the prospect.
8. Be honest and direct.
9. Match communication style to the prospect without manipulating them.
10. Price is only properly evaluated after relevant value has been established.
11. Never manufacture urgency, scarcity, authority, proof, or results.
12. Never make unsupported health, income, legal, or performance claims.
13. Respect a clear rejection or request to stop communication.
14. Record relevant outcomes and next steps in the approved CRM or operating system.
15. Every qualified conversation ends with a specific next action.

## 7. Communication Style Adaptation

Representatives should identify how the prospect prefers to receive and evaluate information.

### Performer

Often responds to energy, possibility, recognition, momentum, and visible outcomes.

Approach:

- Keep the conversation engaging.
- Connect the solution to the prospect’s vision.
- Show what progress could look like.
- Maintain structure so excitement becomes action.

### Analyzer

Often responds to evidence, logic, details, process, and risk reduction.

Approach:

- Be precise.
- Explain the process clearly.
- Provide relevant facts and assumptions.
- Avoid exaggerated promises or pressure.

### Controller

Often responds to speed, confidence, autonomy, efficiency, and measurable results.

Approach:

- Be direct.
- Respect their time.
- Focus on outcomes, control, and execution.
- Present clear choices and next steps.

### Empathizer

Often responds to trust, support, stability, understanding, and relationships.

Approach:

- Listen carefully.
- Acknowledge concerns.
- Explain the support structure.
- Avoid rushing the decision.

These profiles are communication guides, not rigid labels. Representatives must not stereotype or manipulate prospects.

## 8. Required AI and CRM Behavior

Any AI sales assistant built for Lion Elite should:

- Begin with discovery rather than an offer.
- Use known prospect information only when lawfully obtained and relevant.
- Generate clarifying questions based on the prospect’s stated situation.
- Recommend solutions only after minimum qualification criteria are met.
- Explain why a recommendation fits.
- Identify missing information and uncertainty.
- Avoid unsupported claims.
- Recognize opt-outs and suppression requirements.
- Escalate sensitive, complex, or high-impact decisions to a human.
- Save the source, date, owner, status, and next action for each qualified lead.
- Learn from approved outcomes without using protected or irrelevant personal characteristics.

## 9. Quality-Control Checklist

Before approving a sales script, automation, or campaign, confirm:

- Does it open with relevance rather than a pitch?
- Does it contain meaningful discovery questions?
- Does it allow the prospect to speak more than the representative?
- Is credibility concise and relevant?
- Is the proposed value connected to an identified goal?
- Is the recommended solution supported by the discovery?
- Are fees or commercial terms presented clearly?
- Is the next step specific?
- Are claims accurate and supportable?
- Are the data source and outreach method lawful and permitted?
- Are consent, suppression, and opt-out requirements addressed?
- Is human review included where appropriate?

If any answer is no, the workflow is not ready for deployment.
