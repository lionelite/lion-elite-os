# Commission Tracking SOP

Source basis: Google Drive commission and sales sheets including `Chanell commission 2026`, `Daylen's Monthly Peptides & Commissions`, `Daylen Sales 2026`, and `Colin 2026`. These sheets track rep, date, customer, total paid, product/quantity, tracking, order status, and commission notes.

## Purpose
Create a clear repeatable process for calculating, reviewing, and paying commissions accurately.

## Required Fields
Each commissionable sale should include:

- Date
- Rep name
- Customer name
- Phone number
- Email if available
- Shipping address if shipped
- Total paid
- Product/quantity
- Product cost if calculating net commission
- Tracking number
- Order status
- Commission percentage
- Commission amount
- Payment status

## Commission Workflow

### 1. Confirm Sale Source
Identify who generated the sale:

- Daylen
- Chanell
- Colin
- Website/direct
- Referral
- Other rep

### 2. Confirm Payment
Only calculate commissions on confirmed paid orders.

Do not pay commission on:

- Pending payments
- Cancelled orders
- Refunded orders
- Unclear payments

### 3. Confirm Product Cost
If commission is based on profit, subtract product cost first.

Formula:

`Net Profit = Total Paid - Product Cost`

`Commission = Net Profit x Commission %`

If commission is based on gross sale, use:

`Commission = Total Paid x Commission %`

### 4. Confirm Fulfillment Status
Before final payment, confirm:

- Order was fulfilled
- Tracking exists if shipped
- Customer issue is not pending

### 5. Record Weekly Commission
Track by week:

- Week date range
- Rep
- Customer sales
- Total sales
- Total product cost
- Net profit
- Commission percentage
- Commission owed
- Paid/unpaid status

## Weekly Review Checklist

- [ ] Review all new sales by rep
- [ ] Confirm payment status
- [ ] Confirm product costs
- [ ] Confirm shipping/tracking
- [ ] Calculate commission
- [ ] Send commission summary to rep
- [ ] Mark as paid when paid

## Rep Summary Template

Hey [Rep], here is your commission breakdown for [Week Range]:

Sales:
- [Customer] — $[Total Paid] — [Products] — Commission: $[Amount]

Total commission owed: $[Total]

Let me know if you see anything that needs review.

## Common Mistakes

- Paying commission before payment clears
- Forgetting product cost
- Mixing gross and net commission rules
- Missing referral sales
- Not updating paid/unpaid status
- Not tracking refunds or customer issues

## Recommended Improvement
Create a single master commission sheet that pulls from all rep sales instead of maintaining separate sheets for each rep.
