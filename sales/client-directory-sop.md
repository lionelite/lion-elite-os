# Client Directory SOP

Source basis: Google Drive `Client directory`, which tracks date, name, phone number, email address, ordered products, status, and notes.

## Purpose
Keep every customer and lead organized so Lion Elite can follow up, re-market, track orders, and build long-term relationships.

## Required Fields
Every client or lead should include:

- Date added
- Name
- Phone number
- Email address
- Product ordered or interest
- Current status
- Notes
- Next follow-up date
- Source

## Status Options

- New Lead
- Reach Out
- Contacted
- Interested
- Paid
- Order Processing
- Shipped
- Delivered
- Follow-Up Needed
- Repeat Buyer
- Not Interested
- Needs Review

## Daily Workflow

### 1. Add New Leads
Any new contact should be added immediately.

Sources:

- Website
- Instagram
- Text
- Referral
- Rep
- In-person
- Previous buyer

### 2. Update After Every Interaction
After a call, text, DM, or order:

- Update status
- Add notes
- Add products discussed
- Set next follow-up date

### 3. Follow Up
Every day, review clients marked:

- Reach Out
- Contacted
- Interested
- Follow-Up Needed
- Repeat Buyer

### 4. Tag Product Interest
Track what each person ordered or asked about.

Examples:

- Retatrutide
- KLOW
- CJC/Ipamorelin
- GHK-Cu
- MOTS-C
- NAD+
- KPV
- BAC Water

## Follow-Up Templates

### New Lead
Hey [Name], this is Alex with Lion Elite. I saw you were interested and wanted to ask what your main goal is right now: fat loss, muscle, energy, performance, or overall wellness?

### Previous Buyer
Hey [Name], checking in on your Lion Elite order. How has everything been going so far?

### Re-Order
Hey [Name], I wanted to check if you needed anything restocked or if you wanted help planning your next order.

### No Response
Hey [Name], just following up. Do you still want help with [goal/product], or should I close this out for now?

## Weekly Review

- [ ] Review all Reach Out clients
- [ ] Review all previous buyers
- [ ] Identify reorder opportunities
- [ ] Identify testimonials/case studies
- [ ] Clean duplicate contacts
- [ ] Update missing emails/phone numbers

## Recommended Improvement
Turn the client directory into a CRM-style sheet with filters for status, product interest, next follow-up date, and rep/source.
