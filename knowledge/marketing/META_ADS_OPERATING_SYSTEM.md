# Lion Elite Meta Ads Operating System

Updated: July 24, 2026

## Mission
Learn Meta Ads by operating real campaigns, turn results into case studies, and build a repeatable client acquisition service.

## How Meta Ads works
Ads Manager has three levels: Campaign (objective/strategy), Ad Set (conversion location, performance goal, audience, placements, budget/schedule as applicable), and Ad (identity, creative, copy, CTA, destination, tracking). Meta's auction optimizes toward the objective/performance goal you select, so optimize for the business outcome whenever it is measurable.

## Objectives
Core objectives: Awareness, Traffic, Engagement, Leads, App Promotion, Sales.
- Awareness: reach, impressions, recall/video awareness.
- Traffic: visits/clicks when the ultimate action cannot be reliably measured. Do not default to Traffic when the real measurable goal is leads or purchases.
- Engagement: messages, video views, post engagement and related actions.
- Leads: forms, website leads, calls or messaging depending on setup; strong for service businesses.
- Sales: measurable purchases/lower-funnel conversions. Install Pixel for website sales measurement/optimization.

Rule: choose the objective closest to the action that makes the business money.

## Tracking foundation
Before scaling website campaigns:
1. Install Meta Pixel.
2. Configure standard/custom events.
3. Implement Conversions API when appropriate.
4. Verify events and deduplication.
5. Test click-to-conversion flow.
6. Add UTMs and reconcile Meta with analytics/CRM.

Ecommerce: ViewContent -> AddToCart -> InitiateCheckout -> Purchase.
Service: LandingPageView -> Lead -> Qualified Lead -> Appointment -> Customer. Feed reliable downstream outcomes back when the stack permits it.

## New service-business campaign architecture
Keep it simple to avoid fragmentation.
- Objective: Leads for inquiries/applications; Sales for measurable purchases.
- Start with 1-2 prospecting ad sets unless segmentation has a clear purpose.
- Test Broad/Advantage+ audience as a serious default.
- Advantage+ placements generally deserve the first test unless placement requirements justify exclusions.
- Begin with 3-6 materially different creatives.

Retarget once warm pools are meaningful: site visitors, Meta engagers, video viewers, leads/non-buyers and permitted customer lists.

## Audience strategy
Advantage+ audience can treat audience inputs as suggestions and expand when Meta predicts better results. Test broad/Advantage+, high-quality first-party custom audiences, lookalikes when source quality/volume supports them, and interests when justified. Exclude existing customers/leads when acquisition economics require it and controls permit.

## Creative system
Test distinct concepts, not five cosmetic variations:
- Problem -> solution
- Desired outcome
- Demonstration
- Founder story
- Customer story/testimonial (permission + compliant claims)
- Objection handling
- Comparison/differentiation
- Education
- Offer/value stack
- Social proof
- FAQ

Video: HOOK -> PROBLEM/DESIRE -> VALUE/MECHANISM -> PROOF -> OFFER -> CTA.
Design mobile-first. Make the opening understandable immediately and video understandable without depending entirely on sound. Meta supports Advantage+ creative enhancements; review generated assets rather than accepting blindly.

## Copy framework
Answer quickly: Why care? What is offered? Why believe it? What next?
HOOK -> PROBLEM/DESIRE -> VALUE -> PROOF -> OFFER -> CTA.
Keep claims truthful, substantiated and compliant.

## Offer engineering
Ads amplify offers; they do not rescue weak ones. Define ICP, pain/desire, outcome, deliverable, differentiator, value/price, truthful risk reversal, genuine urgency/scarcity, and CTA. Reduce unnecessary lead-form friction while retaining enough qualification to protect lead quality.

## Budget/testing
No universal magic daily budget exists. Budget must generate meaningful conversion information relative to expected CPA. Meta recommends sufficient budget over at least seven days to allow learning. Daily budgets are averages; lifetime budgets cap total campaign spend.

Testing rule: spend enough to answer a question and avoid changing multiple major variables simultaneously. Test creative concepts, audience strategy, offer, and landing page with a written hypothesis.

## Metrics
Business: Revenue, gross profit, CAC, ROAS, LTV, qualified-lead rate, lead-to-customer rate, blended efficiency/MER where useful.
Diagnostic: Spend, impressions, reach, frequency, CPM, link CTR, CPC, landing-page views, CPL, CPA/CAC, conversion rate, ROAS.
Formulas:
- CPM = spend / impressions * 1,000
- CTR = link clicks / impressions
- CPC = spend / link clicks
- CPL = spend / leads
- CAC = spend / acquired customers under chosen attribution methodology
- ROAS = attributed revenue / ad spend
Cheap CPL is not success if leads do not qualify or buy.

## Diagnosis
High CPM: investigate competition, audience constraints, relevance/creative, placements and seasonality.
Low CTR: investigate hook, concept, message-market match and offer presentation.
Good CTR + poor page conversion: inspect speed, message match, trust, UX, offer, friction and tracking.
Many leads + poor qualification: improve signals, pre-qualification, questions and message alignment.
Qualified leads + poor sales: inspect response time, follow-up, show rate, sales process and offer.
Good economics + low volume: test more creative, broader delivery, budget and funnel capacity.

## Scaling
Vertical: increase budget on proven campaigns while monitoring marginal economics.
Horizontal: expand creative concepts, offers, geographies/audiences or funnels. Advantage+ campaign budget can distribute spend across ad sets based on opportunities. Never scale from one lucky day.

## Retargeting
When pools justify it, segment by intent: engagers/video viewers, site visitors, high-intent visitors, leads/non-buyers, cart/checkout abandonment. Warm creative should handle objections, add proof and drive the next action. Monitor frequency/fatigue.

## Lead operations
Advertising and sales are one system. Route leads to CRM immediately; confirm receipt; respond quickly; track attempts, qualification, booking, show/no-show, won/lost and revenue. Feed quality outcomes back when technically supported.

## Landing-page checklist
Message match; clear headline/offer above fold; one dominant CTA; mobile optimization; speed; proof; objection handling; minimal friction; verified tracking; working confirmation event.

## A/B testing
Write hypothesis, primary KPI and decision rule first. Change one major variable when clean causal comparison matters. Maintain a test log.

## Weekly dashboard
Spend; leads/purchases; qualified leads; appointments; customers; revenue; CPL; qualified CPL; CAC; ROAS; funnel conversion rates; best/worst concepts; learning; next changes. Report business outcomes, not vanity metrics.

## Lion Elite case-study process
Use Lion Elite Beauty as the primary conventional coaching/lead-generation training environment, subject to Meta policy and applicable advertising law.
1. Instrument: access, Page/IG, Pixel/CAPI, events, UTMs, CRM.
2. Offer: one customer, one offer, one application/landing path.
3. Creative: at least five distinct concepts.
4. Launch: simple Lead campaign; sufficient budget/time; avoid constant edits.
5. Optimize: diagnose full funnel, not only CTR/CPL.
6. Document: screenshots, weekly data, hypotheses, changes, lessons.
7. Case study: business -> problem -> baseline -> strategy -> creative -> funnel -> spend -> leads -> qualified leads -> customers -> revenue -> CAC/ROAS -> lessons.

## Agency service to build
Sell measurable acquisition, not 'boosted posts': offer/funnel audit, tracking QA, campaign build, creative strategy, copy, production coordination, landing-page recommendations, optimization, lead-quality review, reporting and testing roadmap. Never guarantee ROAS. Clearly separate agency-controlled variables from client-controlled offer, sales, fulfillment and pricing.

## Client onboarding
Collect business/revenue goal, AOV/deal value, margin, historical CAC/CPL, close rate, LTV, geography, ICP, data permissions, Meta access, site access as needed, Pixel/dataset status, analytics/CRM, assets, proof permissions, brand guide, offers/pricing, and legal/compliance restrictions.

## Common mistakes
Boosting as the whole strategy; Traffic objective for measurable lead/sales goals; optimizing clicks over outcomes; too many ad sets on small budgets; constant edits; tiny creative variations only; ignoring page conversion/lead quality/revenue; relying on Meta-attributed ROAS alone; scaling past operational capacity; deceptive or unsupported claims.

## Operating loop
Research -> Offer -> Creative -> Tracking -> Launch -> Measure -> Diagnose -> Test -> Scale -> Document.
