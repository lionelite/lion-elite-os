# Brand Positioning

## Lion Elite Wellness

**Status:** Durable operating context.

- Positioning: premium research and education brand.
- Customer-facing peptide content must remain Research-Use-Only.
- Do not provide dosing, reconstitution, administration, human-use instructions, treatment guidance, or unsupported medical claims.
- Product-specific creative must use the exact approved vial/product asset when available.
- Customer experience should feel premium, established, high-trust, and professionally designed.
- Marketing should optimize ethically for the next valuable action while preserving compliance and trust.

Default footer when appropriate:

`For research purposes only. Not for human consumption.`

## Lion Elite Beauty

**Status:** Durable operating context.

- Positioning: premium coaching and transformation support.
- Lead with accountability, structure, personalized guidance, execution, and premium customer experience.
- Do not position Lion Elite Beauty as generic low-cost information or commodity coaching.
- Primary CTA should usually be an application, consultation, or premium coaching conversation.

## AlexTheLionLifts

**Status:** Owner-established personal brand direction.

- Positioning: founder authority, training, discipline, athletic development, consistency, leadership, and personal execution.
- Content should build trust and founder equity while feeding relevant Lion Elite demand naturally.
- Avoid making every post a direct product pitch.

## Cross-brand experience standard

Every customer-facing touchpoint should feel intentional and premium rather than like a plain message. Use clear hierarchy, strong presentation, credible specificity, a single dominant CTA where appropriate, and language that reflects an established company rather than a desperate seller.
