## Naming Rules

### Rule 1: Use One Name Everywhere
Do not use multiple spellings for the same product.

Bad examples:
- Reta
- reta 10
- Retatrutide 10mg
- Retatrutide

Standard example:
- Retatrutide 10mg

### Rule 2: Include Dosage When Needed
For product tracking, include dosage when there are multiple versions.

Example:
- NAD+ 500mg
- NAD+ 1000mg

### Rule 3: Keep Sales Sheets Clean
Product/Quantity should be written clearly.

Example:
`1 Retatrutide 10mg | 1 KLOW80 80mg | 1 BAC Water`
