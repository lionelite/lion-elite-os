# Lion Elite Content Command Center

Live Google Sheet: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/152g0is6paUiAYwyWiVBJ-miGvh9OUOUnrxOMS84aZYo

## Purpose
This file is the GitHub operating reference for the Lion Elite content engine. The Google Sheet is the live production calendar, trend radar, SEO map, creator brief library, and analytics workspace.

## North Star
Build a high-volume, education-first media system for Lion Elite Wellness and a creator-driven beauty and lifestyle media system for Lion Elite Beauty.

## Daily Production Target
- 4 Wellness master assets
- 3 Beauty master assets
- 1 cross-brand founder or UGC master asset
- 8 master assets per day
- 240 master assets per 30-day cycle before repurposing

## Content Flywheel
Research and SEO -> high-hook creative -> publish and distribute -> measure performance -> identify winners -> creator variations and paid candidates -> feed results back into the Content Brain.

## Trend Refresh Rules
- Review current Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Google/search, and relevant competitor patterns.
- Cross-reference trends with Lion Elite's actual social performance before changing the calendar.
- Do not copy trends blindly. Adapt only trends that fit the brand, audience, and compliance requirements.
- Add new ideas to the Google Sheet Trend Radar with source URL and a 1-10 trend score.
- Replace weak upcoming concepts only when the replacement has a stronger hook, clearer audience fit, or meaningful evidence from current platform behavior.
- Preserve high-performing evergreen series even when short-lived trends change.

## Performance Feedback Loop
Track every published asset by brand, topic, format, hook type, creator, CTA, and URL. Measure views, reach, watch time, retention, saves, shares, comments, follows, clicks, attributable revenue, and organic score. Mark top performers as repurpose and/or ad candidates.

## SEO Integration
Every major social topic should map to a search cluster and, where useful, a canonical website resource. Use clear searchable titles rather than vague clickbait. Keep the social calendar and website education roadmap connected.

## Creator / Affiliate System
Customer -> UGC Creator -> Affiliate -> Elite Affiliate -> Paid Creator -> Brand Ambassador.

Creator briefs should include hook options, talking points, approved language, prohibited claims, CTA, product visuals/B-roll, source references, and examples of winning content. Creator performance should feed back into future briefs.

## Publishing Guardrail
The calendar is a production and approval system. Content should not be blindly auto-published because a trend is popular. Accuracy, platform compliance, and brand fit remain required.
