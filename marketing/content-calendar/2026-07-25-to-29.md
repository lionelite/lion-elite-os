## Source-of-truth rules
This campaign inherits all requirements from `docs/marketing-content-engine-v2.md`.

### Mandatory asset/topic identity gate
Before any post reaches Metricool:
1. `brand` must equal `Lion Elite Wellness`.
2. `campaign_id`, `content_id`, `topic`, `caption`, `visual`, and `CTA` must all describe the same subject.
3. A compound-specific vial/image may appear only when the visible label exactly matches the compound in the content metadata.
4. If the content is mechanism/testing/scientific-literacy focused, prefer non-compound-specific laboratory/research visuals or a neutral Lion Elite Wellness research vial.
5. Never substitute a random vial to fill a media slot.
6. Run text-vs-image validation before scheduling. A mismatch is a hard block.
7. TikTok photo assets must be JPEG/JPG/WebP; PNG is blocked before Metricool.
8. Required footer on LEW public assets/captions where appropriate: `For research and educational purposes only. Not for human consumption.`

### LinkedIn caption
The fastest way to misunderstand peptide research is to read the conclusion and skip the study design.

Before repeating a claim, ask:

- Was the work in vitro, animal, or human research?
- What population or model was studied?
- What endpoint was measured?
- How large was the study?
- What limitations did the authors identify?

Scientific literacy beats hype every time.
