# DM and SMS Follow-Up System

## Purpose
Turn social engagement into conversations, consultations, and customers.

---

# New Lead DM

Hey [Name], I saw you were interested in Lion Elite. What are you focused on most right now: fat loss, muscle, energy, recovery, or performance?

---

# After They Answer

That makes sense. What have you already tried, and what do you feel is not working?

---

# Consultation Invite

Based on what you said, the best next step is a quick consultation so we can understand your goal, current routine, and where the bottleneck is. Do you want me to send you the next step?

---

# No Response Follow-Up 1

Hey [Name], just following up. Are you still looking to improve [goal], or should I close this out for now?

---

# No Response Follow-Up 2

No pressure at all. I just do not want you stuck guessing if this is something you still want help with. Want me to send you where to start?

---

# Previous Buyer Check-In

Hey [Name], checking in on your Lion Elite order. How has everything been going so far?

---

# Reorder Message

Hey [Name], wanted to check if you needed anything restocked or if you wanted help planning your next order.

---

# Story Poll Follow-Up

Hey [Name], I saw you answered the poll. What is the main thing you are trying to improve right now?

---

# Consultation Reminder

Hey [Name], reminder for your Lion Elite consultation today at [time]. Come ready with your main goal and what you feel has been holding you back.

---

# Post-Consultation Follow-Up

Good speaking with you today. Based on what you told me, the main focus is [goal]. The next step is [next step]. Do you want to move forward today?
