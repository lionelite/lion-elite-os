# Meta Ads Launch + Optimization Checklist

## Before build
- Business goal and conversion defined
- Target CAC/CPL derived from economics
- Offer and CTA approved
- Landing destination tested on mobile
- Meta Business assets/access verified
- Pixel/events tested
- CAPI/deduplication checked where used
- CRM/lead routing tested
- UTMs/source tracking defined
- Existing customer/lead exclusions decided where appropriate
- Creative rights/permissions confirmed
- Claims/policy review complete

## Campaign build
- Objective matches measurable outcome
- Naming convention consistent
- Geography correct
- Budget and schedule correct
- Audience strategy documented
- Placements documented
- Optimization event correct
- 3-6+ materially distinct initial concepts when budget supports it
- Correct Page/Instagram identity
- Primary text/headline/CTA proofread
- Destination URL correct
- Tracking parameters correct
- Every placement preview checked

## Launch QA
Click each ad -> page loads -> form/checkout works -> event fires -> CRM receives lead/order -> source captured -> confirmation works -> team notification works.

## First checks
Look for delivery failures, disapprovals, broken links, event/tracking anomalies, budget errors and lead-routing failures. Do not make emotional edits because of a few hours of noisy performance.

## Optimization hierarchy
1. Tracking broken? Fix it.
2. Offer fundamentally weak? Fix offer/message.
3. Creative not earning attention/action? Iterate concepts/hooks.
4. Clicks but no conversions? Diagnose page/funnel.
5. Leads but low quality? Improve message/qualification/signal.
6. Qualified leads but no sales? Diagnose sales process.
7. Profitable and stable? Scale deliberately while expanding creative supply.

## Winner protocol
When a creative wins: document why it may work; preserve original; create super-iterations; change one meaningful component at a time when learning matters; test translations into other formats; watch marginal economics and fatigue.

## Loser protocol
Do not merely delete and forget. Record: hook, angle, format, audience, spend/data level, outcome, likely failure point, what it teaches and whether a new iteration is justified.

## Weekly decision log
Campaign | Ad/ad set | Observation | Evidence | Hypothesis | Action | Date | Owner | Expected result | Review date | Actual result | Learning.

## Core dashboard
Spend | Impressions | CPM | Link CTR | CPC | LPV | Leads/Purchases | CVR | CPL/CPA | Qualified Leads | Bookings | Shows | Customers | Revenue | CAC | ROAS | Gross Profit when available.

## Rule
Meta metrics diagnose. Business economics decide.
