# Marketing Agency Client Sales + Onboarding SOP

## Goal
Turn qualified businesses into well-instrumented advertising clients without overpromising results.

## Discovery call
Ask in this order:
1. What are you selling and what is the primary offer?
2. What is the average sale/deal value?
3. What are gross margins/variable fulfillment costs?
4. How many customers/leads do you get monthly now and from where?
5. What has been spent on Meta before? Results?
6. What percentage of qualified leads close?
7. What is repeat purchase/LTV when known?
8. Where can you serve/sell?
9. What makes customers choose you?
10. What proof/assets/testimonials exist?
11. What happens after a lead arrives and how fast is response?
12. What monthly ad budget can the business sustain for a meaningful test?
13. What would make the next 90 days successful?

## Qualification
Green: clear offer, real demand/proof, workable economics, responsive owner/team, fulfillment capacity, access to tracking, realistic expectations.
Yellow: weak funnel, limited creative, uncertain close rate, poor tracking. Sell/perform setup and measurement work first.
Red: deceptive claims, guaranteed-return expectation, no ability to fulfill, refuses measurement/access, economics cannot plausibly support acquisition.

## Proposal structure
Business goal -> diagnosed bottleneck -> scope -> first 30-day implementation -> ongoing testing/optimization -> reporting -> client responsibilities -> agency responsibilities -> management fee -> ad spend paid by client -> term/cancellation -> no performance guarantee.

## Positioning
We are not selling button-clicking in Ads Manager. We manage the acquisition system: offer/message, creative strategy, campaigns, funnel recommendations, tracking, lead-quality feedback and optimization.

## Client responsibilities
Accurate product/service information; timely approvals; legal/compliance accuracy; ad spend; fulfillment; sales follow-up; CRM outcome data; permissions for testimonials/assets; website/CRM access when required.

## Agency responsibilities
Strategy; setup/build within scope; creative briefs/copy; campaign management; tracking QA within scope; documented tests; reporting; communication; responsible budget management; escalation of tracking/policy/business problems.

## Kickoff checklist
Signed agreement/payment; Meta access; Page/IG; ad account; Pixel/dataset; website; analytics; CRM; calendar/forms; creative library; brand guide; offers/pricing; customer research; historical campaign data; margins; sales process; testimonial permissions; primary contacts; approval SLA.

## First-week deliverables
Economics sheet; measurement map; funnel audit; competitor/winner research; campaign architecture; creative matrix; launch checklist; reporting baseline.

## Retention
Clients stay when business outcomes improve and communication is clear. Weekly: what happened, why we think it happened, what we changed, what we need from client, what is next. Monthly: business-level review, not a vanity-metric deck.

## Case-study permission
Ask permission after meaningful verified results. Use exact timeframe and numbers; disclose material context; never imply results are guaranteed for others.
