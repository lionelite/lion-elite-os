# Lion Elite Marketing Department

## Primary Goal
Build Lion Elite to $100,000/month through consistent content, lead generation, trust-building, conversion systems, and repeat-customer growth.

## Brand Separation

### Lion Elite Wellness
Research product storefront. Content should remain research-focused, professional, educational, and compliant with stated positioning.

### Lion Elite Beauty
Wellness, coaching, biomarkers, performance, lifestyle, and client acquisition. This is the primary paid-social and consultation funnel brand.

## Marketing Machine
Every piece of content should move through this workflow:

1. Idea
2. Script
3. Film / Design
4. Caption
5. Post
6. Repurpose
7. Measure
8. Improve

## Daily Posting Standard
Minimum daily output:

- 1 Reel / Short
- 1 Story sequence
- 1 educational post or carousel
- 1 CTA or conversation starter

## Weekly Content Mix

- 40% Education
- 20% Authority / behind the scenes
- 15% Lifestyle / founder brand
- 15% Social proof
- 10% Offer / CTA
