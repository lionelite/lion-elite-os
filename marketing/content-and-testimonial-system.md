## Testimonial Capture Workflow

### 1. Identify Candidate
Look for:

- Repeat buyers
- Clients with visible progress
- Positive text messages
- People participating in content
- People using the program consistently

### 2. Ask for Testimonial
Message:

Hey [Name], your progress has been great to see. Would you be open to sending a quick testimonial about your experience so far? It can be simple: what you were struggling with, what changed, and what you would tell someone thinking about starting.

### 3. Save Testimonial
Save in:

`marketing/testimonials/`

Use naming format:

`client-name-date-testimonial.md`

### 4. Turn Into Content
Each testimonial can become:

- Reel
- Carousel
- Story
- Email
- Website section
- Case study
- Sales proof point
