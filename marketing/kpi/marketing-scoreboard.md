# Marketing Scoreboard

## Purpose
Track whether marketing is moving Lion Elite toward $100,000/month.

## Daily Metrics

- Total website visitors
- Lion Elite Wellness visitors
- Lion Elite Beauty visitors
- Instagram profile visits
- TikTok profile visits
- New followers
- DMs received
- Consultations booked
- Orders placed
- Revenue

## Weekly Metrics

- Total posts published
- Total Reels published
- Total carousel posts published
- Total stories posted
- Total email sends
- Top-performing post
- Lowest-performing post
- Best CTA
- Best content theme
- Website conversion rate
- Consultation conversion rate
- Revenue by source

## Content Performance Fields

For every post, track:

- Date
- Platform
- Brand
- Content pillar
- Hook
- CTA
- Views
- Likes
- Comments
- Saves
- Shares
- Profile visits
- Link clicks
- DMs
- Leads
- Sales

## Weekly Review Questions

1. Which content created the most conversations?
2. Which content drove website visits?
3. Which content created leads?
4. Which CTA performed best?
5. Which content should be remade?
6. What should we stop posting?
7. What should we double down on?

## $100k/Month Math

Example paths:

- 50 clients at $2,000/month = $100,000
- 100 clients at $1,000/month = $100,000
- 500 product orders at $200 average = $100,000
- Mixed model: products + coaching + biomarkers + reorders

## Daily CEO Question
What is the highest-leverage action today that creates more qualified leads, more consultations, more orders, or more repeat customers?
