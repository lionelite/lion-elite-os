# Truth-First Content Framework

## Positioning

We do not sell validation. We help founders confront the constraint they can no longer afford to avoid.

The tone must be direct, calm, respectful, and specific. The goal is not to humiliate people or perform toughness. The goal is to make honesty feel safe enough to act on.

## Content pillars

### 1. Comfortable truth
Name a hard reality without attacking the person.

Formula: `You are not broken. But the current behavior is producing the current result.`

### 2. Ownership without shame
Separate identity from behavior.

Formula: `This is not proof that you cannot scale. It is evidence that the present system cannot.`

### 3. Evidence over ego
Challenge unsupported certainty.

Formula: `Confidence is useful. Numbers are more useful.`

### 4. Execution filter
Make implementation the standard.

Formula: `The right client does not need to agree instantly. They need to test, measure, and adjust.`

### 5. Standards before sales
Disqualify poor-fit prospects publicly.

Formula: `If you want agreement, we are not the right partner. If you want clarity and execution, keep listening.`

## Approved hooks

- Your business may not need another strategy. It may need a founder willing to stop protecting the wrong one.
- We will never use your ambition as an excuse to avoid the numbers.
- The truth should not feel cruel. It should feel useful.
- You do not need less confidence. You need confidence that can survive evidence.
- The best founders are not ego-free. They are willing to put the mission above the ego.
- We are not impressed by talent that refuses accountability.
- A hard conversation handled well can save six months of wasted execution.

## Disqualification language

Use behavior-based language:

- Not a fit when feedback consistently becomes debate instead of implementation.
- Not a fit when the founder wants guarantees but will not provide accurate numbers.
- Not a fit when every missed target is explained by the market, team, agency, or audience.
- Not a fit when accountability is treated as disrespect.

Do not use insults, amateur diagnoses, humiliation, or claims that someone has a personality disorder.

## CTA pattern

`Apply only if you are willing to bring the real numbers, hear the real constraint, and execute the agreed plan.`
